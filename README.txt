CHOCOLUX THREE-PAGE WEBSITE
===========================

FILES
- index.html       Home page
- products.html    Product catalog with category filters
- about.html       About the business and contact form
- styles.css       Shared design and responsive styles
- script.js        Mobile menu, WhatsApp order buttons, product filters, and contact form

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the chocolux_website folder.
3. Double-click index.html to view the website in your browser.
4. Keep all files together in the same folder.

IMPORTANT CUSTOMIZATION
1. Open script.js in a text editor.
2. Replace the placeholder CHOCOLUX_WHATSAPP number with your real WhatsApp number.
   Use country code only, with no +, spaces, or dashes.
   Example format for Pakistan: 923001234567
3. In index.html and products.html, replace example product details such as
   "Pack size: Add details · Price: Add price" with your real sizes and prices.
4. Update the contact information in about.html.
5. Review product photos and replace any that do not match your actual products.
   The current image links use remote images and need an internet connection.
6. Update the story and quality claims to reflect your actual business.

ORDERING
The site opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message after you add your number.
It does not include a payment gateway, cart, inventory system, or server-side form
storage. Those can be added if you later want a full online store.

PUBLISHING
You can upload the files to a static hosting service or your web hosting account.
Use index.html as the homepage. Test all three pages and the WhatsApp links before
publishing the site publicly.


IMAGE UPDATE: The HTML image links have been updated to show dates and date-based products more clearly. To update GitHub Pages, upload/commit the updated index.html, products.html, and about.html (or upload the full folder files) to the chocolux repository and wait for Pages to redeploy.
